A SEPARATE HOUSEHOLD CALCULATOR

Transparent arithmetic, household by household.

Share in year k = 12 × monthly cost × (1 + cost growth)k / [annual income × (1 + income growth)k]. These are assumed household quantities, not county predictions.

What the target measures

Renter households spending at least 30% of household income on gross rent, among households with a computable ratio. ACS B25070 categories 007–010 divided by total minus “not computed.” A ratio of medians is a different statistic.

Three kinds of uncertainty

ACS margins describe sampling uncertainty. Residual-calibrated intervals describe prediction uncertainty under historical conditions. Ablations, held-out states and scenarios describe sensitivity to assumptions. These answer different questions.

What this study cannot establish

Thirty populous counties in three selected states do not represent the United States. Current revised downloads do not reconstruct historical vintages. Three geographic groups provide limited independent evidence. No model here identifies a causal effect of growth on rent.

More states, archived vintages, richer demographic controls and independent future years could change the conclusion. ACS 2020 remains missing. Rental vacancy and full interstate destination rankings are excluded.

Feature dictionary and mathematical objective

Minimize Σ(y − a − z′β − γcounty)² + λ(‖β‖² + ‖γ‖²). Predict an unseen county with γ = 0. All imputation and scaling use fitting rows only. No future-year categorical effect is fitted.

Boosting: 80 iterations, learning rate 0.06, L2 = 10, minimum leaf size 15; tune 3 or 7 leaves on earlier temporal folds. Ridge λ ∈ {1, 10, 100}. All outputs clipped to [0,100].
